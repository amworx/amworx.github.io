fxx

A manual companion for gold (XAUUSD) day traders. fxx helps you prepare a session, size your risk, respect the economic calendar, and review your own decisions afterwards. It is a notebook and a calculator — not a signal service.

What it does

Built for the working day

  • Live board and session clock

    The current XAUUSD mid price with the day's change, day range position and spread, alongside which session is open — Asia, London, New York — in the display time zone you choose.

  • Pre-trade checklist

    A short, fixed list you run through before every entry, so the decision is made before the position is, not after.

  • Risk calculator in R-multiples

    Position size from your account balance and stop distance, with stop and target levels in dollars and pips, and the resulting R multiple for the trade you are about to take.

  • Economic blackout guard

    High-impact USD events for the week, so you can see the windows where trading is a coin toss and stay out of them on purpose.

  • Trade journal and R-based statistics

    Log your own trades and review expectancy in R. fxx reports expectancy rather than win rate, because win rate alone says nothing about survival.

  • Local price alerts

    Price levels that notify you when they are reached, running on the device. No server is involved.

  • Display time zone

    Every clock and timestamp in the app is printed in a zone you pick — your device, New York, UTC, or a fixed offset — while the underlying calculations stay in UTC.

What it does not do

No calls, ever

  • No trading signals, no buy or sell recommendations, no “now” moments.
  • No voting, consensus, sentiment or crowd scores.
  • No auto-trading and no broker connection — fxx never places, modifies or closes an order.
  • No promise, projection or implication of profit. Price data is shown as received from public feeds.
  • No financial, investment or tax advice. You remain the only decision-maker.

Trading gold with leverage carries a high risk of losing money. fxx is a record-keeping and arithmetic tool; it cannot make a strategy profitable, and it will not tell you what to do.

Your data

Local first, by design

  • Your journal, calculator inputs, alerts and settings are stored on your device only. fxx v1 has no user database and no sync server.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no profiling. Nothing you type into fxx is sold or shared.
  • You can use the whole app as a guest, without signing in. Signing in with Google is optional and currently only identifies you for a planned future sync feature; it does not move your data anywhere today.
  • Public data feeds are requested without your personal details (the request carries your IP address and a generic app user-agent, as any web request does).
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes everything fxx holds locally.
