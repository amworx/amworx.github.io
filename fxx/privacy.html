1. Summary

fxx is a manual trading companion. It keeps your notes, calculations and settings on your own device. There is no fxx account server: version 1 has no backend, no user database and no sync. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app receives your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture) from Google and stores it locally so it can greet you and, in a future version, sync your data at your request.

We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind in the app, and we do not sell or share your information.

2. What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, your email address and your profile picture. These come from Google's basic identity scopes (openid, email, profile). fxx requests nothing else from your Google Account — no contacts, no Drive, no calendar, no Gmail.
  • Whatever you enter in the app: journal entries, calculator inputs, checklist state, price alerts and settings. This is written to local storage on your device.
  • Nothing else. The app does not read your contacts, messages, files, photos or location.

Because fxx v1 has no server, the developer cannot see your journal, your balance, your alerts or your trades. There is nowhere for them to be sent.

3. Google sign-in and Google user data

Signing in is optional. If you tap “Continue with Google”, the authentication itself is performed by Google on your device; fxx never sees your Google password. Google's handling of that interaction is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We use the basic profile information we receive for exactly two purposes:

  • to show which account you are signed in as inside the app; and
  • to identify your data for a future, opt-in cross-device sync feature.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, for building profiles, for creditworthiness or lending decisions, or for training machine-learning models. We do not transfer it to third parties, and no human reads it — there is no server that holds it.

fxx's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke fxx's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by signing out inside the app, which deletes the locally stored profile information.

4. Data stored on your device

Journal entries, calculator inputs, alerts, preferences (including your chosen display time zone and account balance you typed) and — if you signed in — your Google name, email address and profile picture are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Other applications on your phone cannot read it, and it is not backed up to us.

Device-level backup settings are controlled by Android and by your device manufacturer, not by fxx; if you have enabled system backup for apps, your device may include fxx's local data in it.

5. Network requests the app makes

fxx is offline-capable, but a few features fetch public data. A request of this kind necessarily reveals your IP address and a generic user-agent string to the host, in the same way as any web page. No account information, journal content or other personal data is included in these requests.

Outbound requests
Host Purpose Sent
biquote.io Live XAUUSD price (primary feed) IP address, generic user-agent
api.gold-api.com Live XAU price (fallback feed) IP address, generic user-agent
financecalendar.com Economic calendar (primary) IP address, generic user-agent
nfs.faireconomy.media Economic calendar (fallback weekly feed) IP address, generic user-agent
Google (accounts.google.com) Sign-in, only if you choose to sign in Handled by Google
A manifest URL, if configured Optional in-app update check IP address, generic user-agent

Tapping a provider's link (for example the calendar attribution in Settings) opens your browser, where that site's own privacy policy applies.

6. What we do not do

  • No analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels.
  • No user accounts on a server, no sync, no cloud copy of your journal in version 1.
  • No selling, renting, licensing or sharing of your information with third parties.
  • No profiling and no automated decision-making about you.
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos, files, messages or call data.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as you keep it there. You are in control of it:

  • Sign out in Settings to remove the stored Google profile information.
  • Clear storage (Android Settings → Apps → fxx → Storage → Clear storage) to delete everything the app holds.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of it at once.

Because no server-side copy exists, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have a question about deletion, write to us at the address in section 10 and we will confirm what the app stores and where.

8. Children

fxx is a trading tool intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help you remove the local data as described above.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with a new “Last updated” date, and the in-app privacy text will be updated to match. Material changes — for example the introduction of the planned sync feature — will be described in the app's release notes before they take effect.
